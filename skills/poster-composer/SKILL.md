# Compose Paper Poster

1. Lead with the paper identity and one concise supported takeaway.
2. Follow the argument chain appropriate to the classified paper type.
3. Give high-priority sections more space; keep supporting sections compact rather than omitting uncertainty.
4. Place each formula, figure, or table beside the claim it supports.
5. Preserve a visible quality-review area for evidence gaps, limitations, and verification status.
6. Avoid long prose, repeated summaries, nested panels, and decorative assets unrelated to the paper.

Use original assets when available and label reconstructed diagrams as explanations rather than paper figures.
